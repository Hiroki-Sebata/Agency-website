Privacy notice

The short version: this site collects nothing unless you fill in the contact form, and we use what you send only to reply to you.

No cookies No analytics No third-party fonts

Last reviewed: October 2026

This is a starting point, not legal advice. 01

Who is responsible for your data

This site is run by Northbound, a studio founded by Miguel Lourenço and Hiroki Sebata. If you want anything on this page explained, or want your data removed, email us using the address at the bottom and a person will reply.

to add Your registered business name, legal form and trading address belong here. If you operate in the UK or EU, this is the “data controller” information people are entitled to.

02

What this site collects

Only what you type into the contact form: your name, your email address, optionally your business name, which service you selected, your indicated timing, and your message.

There is nothing else. This site sets no cookies, runs no analytics, embeds no social media widgets and loads no third-party fonts or trackers — the fonts are served from this domain. Nothing happens in the background while you read.

03

Why we collect it, and on what basis

To reply to you. That is the entire purpose. Under UK and EU data protection law this is either our legitimate interest in responding to a business enquiry, or the first step in performing a contract with you — and in either case you gave it to us deliberately by ticking the consent box.

We will not add you to a mailing list, we will not send you anything you did not ask for, and we will not sell or share your details with anyone for marketing.

04

Who else is involved

Two companies necessarily touch your message on its way to us:

Web3Forms receives the form submission and forwards it to our inbox. GitHub Pages serves this website and, like any web server, processes your IP address in its ordinary access logs. Our email provider then stores the message in our inbox.

to check Confirm the current privacy terms of whichever form service and email provider you end up using, and name them here. If you later add analytics, booking embeds or a chat widget, they must be listed too — and a cookie banner probably becomes necessary.

05

How long we keep it

Enquiries that do not turn into work are deleted within twelve months. If we end up working together, your details stay for as long as the business relationship lasts plus whatever period our accountant and local tax law require us to keep records.

You can ask us to delete your enquiry sooner and we will, unless we are legally required to keep a record of it.

06

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, ask us to delete it, or object to us holding it at all. Email us and we will act on it — there is no form to fill in and no fee.

If you are in the UK or EU and think we have handled your data badly, you are entitled to complain to your national data protection authority. We would rather you told us first so we can fix it.

07

Cookies

This site sets none. Not analytics cookies, not preference cookies, not the kind that follow you around the internet — which is why there is no banner interrupting you.

If that changes, this page changes with it and you will be asked for consent before anything non-essential is stored on your device.

08

Changes to this notice

If we change how any of this works, we will update this page and change the date at the top. Material changes to anything affecting an active client are also sent by email rather than left here quietly.
