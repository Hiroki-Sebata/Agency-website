01

Get found, and get enquiries

You are good at the work. The problem is that the people searching for it are finding somebody worse.

Typical: 3–5 weeks

Built from

Websites & landing pages

Talk about this

Sound familiar?

  • ×You rank below competitors you know do a worse job
  • ×The site reads like a brochure and never actually asks for anything
  • ×Every lead arrives through word of mouth, so growth is luck
  • ×You could not say which page an enquiry came from

What we build

  • A fast site with one clear action per page, written in the language your customers use
  • The pages that answer what buyers ask before they are willing to call
  • Technical SEO groundwork: structure, titles, schema, speed, a sitemap search engines accept
  • Analytics and form tracking, so the next decision is based on something

What changes. Enquiries start arriving with context instead of “how much?”, and you can see which page produced them.

Online storesSupport automation

Talk about this

Sound familiar?

  • ×Orders come in as DMs, comments and “can you send an invoice”
  • ×Stock lives in your head, so you oversell and apologise
  • ×Every order needs you to do something manually
  • ×You cannot say which product actually makes money

What we build

  • A storefront on Shopify, or a custom build if your catalogue is genuinely unusual
  • Checkout and payments that work first try on a phone
  • Stock, variants, shipping zones and collection options set up for real conditions
  • Order, dispatch and refund emails in your voice, sent without you

What changes. Orders arrive already paid, stock reduces itself, and dispatch emails go out at 6am without anybody opening a laptop.

03

Let people book themselves in

Half the week goes on agreeing times, and you still get no-shows.

Typical: 2–4 weeks

Built from

Websites & landing pagesWeb & mobile apps

Talk about this

Sound familiar?

  • ×Booking means four messages back and forth about availability
  • ×No-shows cost you slots you could have sold
  • ×Two people occasionally get the same slot
  • ×The phone rings while you are mid-job

What we build

  • Self-serve booking driven by your real availability, not a guess
  • Deposits or card-on-file at the point of booking, if you want them
  • Automatic reminders by email or text, which is what actually cuts no-shows
  • Two-way calendar sync and one-click rescheduling links

What changes. Bookings happen at 11pm while you are asleep, reminders go out on their own, and the diary stops being a negotiation.

04

Answer customers around the clock

The same ten questions, every single day, answered by whoever happens to be least busy.

Typical: 2–4 weeks

Built from

Support automation & AI

Talk about this

Sound familiar?

  • ×Opening hours, delivery and “where is my order” make up most of your inbox
  • ×Anyone who messages after 6pm hears nothing until tomorrow
  • ×Answers differ depending on who replied
  • ×Someone senior is doing work a well-written page could do

What we build

  • An assistant trained on your own documents, prices and policies — not the open internet
  • The channels you already use: site chat, email, WhatsApp, Instagram
  • Escalation rules so money, complaints and anything unusual reach a human immediately
  • A log of every conversation, which quietly becomes your best customer research

What changes. Most questions are answered in seconds, at any hour, and your team only sees the ones that genuinely need judgement.

05

Replace the spreadsheet everything depends on

Six spreadsheets. Two of them out of date. One of them is the real one, and only one person knows which.

Typical: 6–10 weeks

Built from

Web & mobile apps

Talk about this

Sound familiar?

  • ×The same data gets retyped into two or three different tools
  • ×Nobody is quite sure which file is current
  • ×Training a new hire means a week of “just ask whoever has been here longest”
  • ×One clever spreadsheet is now load-bearing and you are afraid of it

What we build

  • One small internal tool with the fields you actually use and nothing you do not
  • Roles and permissions, so people see what they need and no more
  • A history of changes, so you can see who altered what and when
  • Connections to the tools you keep, so data stops being copied by hand

What changes. There is one place that is true. New staff can be shown it in an hour instead of inheriting folklore.

Get a new idea in front of real users

Typical: 6–10 weeks

Built from

Web & mobile appsWebsites & landing pages

Talk about this

Sound familiar?

  • ×Every estimate so far has been six figures and nine months
  • ×The feature list has grown every week since you thought of it
  • ×You cannot tell which part people would actually pay for
  • ×Deciding has been easier to postpone than to do

What we build

  • A deliberately small first version — the one thing that proves or kills the idea
  • The riskiest assumption tested first, not the easiest one
  • Built so it can be thrown away without regret if the answer is no
  • A measurement plan agreed before launch, so “it went okay” is not the verdict

What changes. In a couple of months you have real behaviour from real users instead of a longer list of opinions.
