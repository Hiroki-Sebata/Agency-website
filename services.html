01

Websites & landing pages

A site whose job is to turn a stranger into an enquiry — fast, written in plain language, and built so you can change the words yourself without calling us.

Typical: 3–5 weeks

We will recommend the platform based on who has to edit the site afterwards — not on what we feel like using.

Start a websites project

What you get

  • Every page designed around one clear thing you want the visitor to do
  • Copy drafted by us, corrected by you — you do not start from a blank page
  • Loads in under two seconds on a phone on mobile data, not just on your laptop
  • The technical SEO groundwork: titles, headings, sitemap, structured data, speed
  • A simple editor if you want to change text and photos yourself
  • Analytics configured so you can see what people actually click

Always included

  • Design reviewed in Figma before code
  • Works from 320px up to large desktops
  • Accessibility pass against WCAG 2.2 AA
  • Enquiry forms with spam filtering
  • Cookie and privacy basics in place
  • Redirects from your old URLs so rankings survive

Typically built with

AstroNext.jsWordPressWebflowTailwindCloudflare

02

Web & mobile apps

Custom software for the part of your business that no off-the-shelf tool fits. Scoped deliberately small, shipped in stages, and documented well enough that a different developer could take it over.

Typical: 6–12 weeks for a first version

If an existing product would do 80% of the job for a fraction of the cost, we will say so before quoting a build.

Start a web project

What you get

  • Accounts, logins and permissions that match how your team is organised
  • The actual workflow your staff follow — not a generic template bent into shape
  • Connections to the tools you already pay for, so data stops being retyped
  • An admin area you can run without us
  • A staging copy, so nothing is tested on real customers
  • Written handover: the repository, the accounts, and how it all fits together

Always included

  • Clickable prototype before build starts
  • Weekly deploys to a link you can open
  • Automated tests on the parts that would hurt most if they broke
  • Error monitoring from day one
  • Plain-English technical documentation
  • Your own cloud accounts, in your name

Typically built with

ReactNext.jsTypeScriptNode.jsPostgresSupabaseReact NativeSwift

03

Online stores

Take money properly. Products, variants, stock, shipping rules, tax and the automatic emails that stop customers emailing you to ask where their order is.

Typical: 4–8 weeks

Most small catalogues belong on Shopify and we will happily tell you that rather than selling you a custom build you do not need.

Start a online stores project

What you get

  • A storefront on Shopify, or a custom build if your catalogue is unusual
  • Checkout and payments that work on a phone, first try
  • Stock that updates itself instead of living in someone's head
  • Shipping zones, rates and collection options set up for real
  • Order, dispatch and refund emails written in your voice
  • Conversion tracking, so ad spend can be judged honestly

Always included

  • Product data imported and tidied
  • Abandoned-cart recovery
  • Discount and bundle rules
  • Tax and invoicing configuration
  • Staff training call plus a short video
  • Launch checklist run together before you go live

Typically built with

ShopifyStripeNext.jsSanityKlaviyoGoogle Analytics

04

Support automation & AI

Answer the questions you have already answered a thousand times, without hiring for it — and keep a person in the loop exactly where it matters.

Typical: 2–4 weeks

We will not let an automation guess at anything that could cost you a customer. Narrow and reliable beats broad and confident.

Start a support automation project

What you get

  • An assistant trained on your documents, prices and policies rather than the open internet
  • Answers on the channels you already use: site chat, email, WhatsApp, Instagram
  • Escalation rules, so money, complaints and anything unusual reach a human
  • An optional approval step: nothing is sent until you press send
  • Every conversation logged, so you can see what customers keep asking for
  • A monthly tune-up as your prices, stock and policies change

Always included

  • A written list of what it will and will not attempt
  • Tone of voice set from your existing replies
  • Hand-off straight into your inbox or CRM
  • Fallback wording for anything it is unsure about
  • Clear note of which provider processes what, so your privacy policy can say so
  • A kill switch you control

Typically built with

OpenAI APIAnthropic APITwilioZapierMakeyour CRM

05

Care & maintenance

Someone who already knows your setup, on hand every month — rather than finding a stranger in a panic the week something breaks.

Typical: Monthly, rolling

Optional. Plenty of clients take the handover pack and run it themselves — that is a perfectly good outcome.

Start a care project

What you get

  • Updates and security patches applied and checked
  • Backups taken and, more importantly, test-restored
  • Uptime and error monitoring, with us notified before you are
  • A set amount of change work each month — new pages, tweaks, fixes
  • A quarterly speed and accessibility check with a short written summary
  • A person who answers email and already knows your project

Always included

  • Rolling monthly, cancel any month
  • Unused change time rolls over one month
  • Response targets agreed in writing
  • No lock-in on hosting or domains
  • Works on projects we did not build, after a review
  • Handover pack kept current, not written in a rush at the end

Typically built with

Uptime monitoringSentryAutomated backupsLighthouse CI

How engagements are structured

Three ways in, depending on how clear the problem is.

We have deliberately left prices off this site. A five-page site and a booking platform with payments are not remotely the same job, and a number invented before we understand yours would only be wrong.

01

Discovery sprint

When the problem is not clear yet

  • One to two weeks of mapping what you actually do today
  • Wireframes or a clickable prototype of the proposed answer
  • A written plan with a fixed price for the build
  • Yours to keep and take to another studio if you prefer
Fixed fee, quoted after a call
02

Fixed-scope project

The usual way we work

  • Scope and price agreed in writing before anything starts
  • Paid in stages tied to milestones, not to hours worked
  • A live link and a written update every week
  • A month of free fixes after launch for anything we got wrong
Fixed price, quoted after a call
03

Monthly care

After it is live

  • Updates, monitoring, backups and security patches
  • A set amount of change work included each month
  • Priority on anything urgent
  • Cancel any month — nothing switches off when you leave
Monthly, cancellable
