What happens next

  1. 01 We read it properly Both of us see every enquiry. You get a reply written by a person, not an autoresponder with a ticket number.
  2. 02 A 20-minute call Video, camera optional. You describe what is going wrong; we ask about five questions and tell you honestly whether we are the right people.
  3. 03 A written plan Within a few days: what we would build, a fixed price, and dates. Yours to keep and take elsewhere if you would rather.

Handy to have ready

  • Your current website address, if you have one
  • Roughly when you need it working by
  • Anything you have already tried that did not stick
  • The question you are most worried about asking

What to expect from us.

You talk for most of it. We ask what the business does, what is currently costing you time or money, and what you have already tried. Then we tell you what we would build and roughly what that costs.

There is no deck and nobody will try to close you. If we are not the right studio for it, we will say so and point you somewhere better.
