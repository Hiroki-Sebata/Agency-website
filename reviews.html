How we collect them

Three questions, every time.

Same questions for every client, asked after the project ends rather than while we are still holding the invoice.

01

What was the problem before we started?

It frames everything else, and it is the sentence a reader recognises themselves in.

02

What is measurably different now?

A number if there is one, an honest “nothing yet” if it is too early.

03

What would you warn the next client about?

We publish these too. A review with no friction in it does not read as real.

Better than reading reviews

Four questions to ask any studio, including us.

Testimonials are chosen by the people they flatter — ours included. If you are deciding between a few studios, these four will tell you more than any page like this one.

Ask us all four on a call

Ask to speak to a past client

Not a quote on a website — an actual conversation. We will put you in touch with someone whose project resembles yours.

Ask to see something live

Any studio can show a mockup. Ask for a URL you can open on your own phone and poke at.

Ask what went wrong on their last project

Everyone has one. The answer tells you far more about how a studio behaves under pressure than any testimonial.

Ask who will actually do the work

At a larger agency the people in the pitch are often not the people who build it. Ask directly, and get the names.
