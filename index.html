Start from the problem

Tell us what is slowing you down, not which technology to use.

You do not have to arrive knowing whether you need an app, a plugin or a rebuild. Find the sentence below that sounds like your week.

Get found, get enquiries

The problem. People search for what you do and find someone else. The site you have reads like a brochure and never asks for the enquiry.

What we build. A fast marketing site built around one action, with the basics search engines actually care about.

Sell online

The problem. Orders arrive as DMs, screenshots and “can you send an invoice”. Stock lives in your head.

What we build. A proper storefront with payments, stock and automatic order emails — on Shopify if that fits, custom if it doesn't.

Take bookings

The problem. Half your week goes on back-and-forth about times, and you still get no-shows.

What we build. Self-serve booking with your real availability, deposits if you want them, and reminders that cut no-shows.

Answer customers 24/7

The problem. The same ten questions, every day, answered by whoever is least busy.

What we build. An assistant trained on your own documents that answers in your words and hands anything delicate to a human.

Replace the spreadsheet

The problem. Six spreadsheets, two of them out of date, one of them the real one.

What we build. One small internal tool with the fields you actually use, so the team stops guessing which tab is current.

Launch a new idea

The problem. You have an idea and no way to find out whether anyone wants it.

What we build. A deliberately small first version in front of real users in weeks, built so it can be thrown away without regret.

In practice

The same ten questions, answered without you.

Opening hours. Do you deliver. Where is my order. Can I move my appointment. None of it needs a person — but all of it needs the right answer, in your words, with a human on hand the moment it gets delicate.

  • Trained on your documents, prices and policies — not the open internet.
  • Answers on the channels you already use: site chat, email, WhatsApp, Instagram.
  • Hands over to you the moment money, complaints or anything unusual comes up.
  • Every conversation logged, so you can see what customers keep asking for.

Before you ask

The questions we get every week.

Every number follows a conversation, because a five-page brochure site and a booking platform with payments are not the same job. What we will commit to: a fixed price agreed before anyone starts, not an hourly meter that creeps.

You get the figure in writing after the first call, along with what is in scope and what is not. That document is yours whether or not you hire us.

Small is the entire point. Big agencies price for big committees — the account manager, the strategist, the deck. We skipped all of that so a one-person shop or a ten-person team can afford senior work.

If your budget genuinely does not fit the problem, we will tell you on the first call and point you at a cheaper route, even when that route is not us.

Yes, completely. The code lives in your repository, the domain is registered to you, and hosting and third-party accounts are created in your name on day one — not ours.

If you stop working with us tomorrow, nothing switches off and nothing needs to be prised out of our hands.

Usually not. Plenty of sites are structurally fine and just badly organised, slow, or silent about what the business actually wants a visitor to do.

We will look at what you have first and say honestly whether it is a rebuild or a rescue. A rescue is cheaper and we are happy to take it.

Expect about an hour a week: one short call to look at progress and answer questions. We do the writing, the design decisions and the technical choices, and bring you the ones that are genuinely yours to make.

What we do need early is access — your domain, your logo files, your current analytics. Chasing those is what actually delays projects.

We watch the site for the first month after it goes live and fix anything that is our fault at no charge. After that you can stay on a monthly care plan or leave it with us on an ad-hoc basis.

Either way you are not locked in, and leaving does not take the site with it.
