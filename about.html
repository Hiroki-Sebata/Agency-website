How we work

Six rules we actually hold ourselves to.

Every studio has a values page. These are written as things we do or refuse to do, so you can hold us to them — and tell us when we have not.

01

Quote the small correct thing

It is more profitable to be recommended than to be fired. If the cheap answer solves it, we will quote the cheap answer.

02

Show work before it is finished

A staging link from day one. Surprises at the end are a failure of process, not a feature of software.

03

Write things down

Scope, decisions and prices in writing. Not because we expect a dispute, but because memory is unreliable and projects run for months.

04

Build boring technology on purpose

We choose well-supported, widely-known tools so the next person to touch your project is not forced into a rewrite.

05

Hand over everything

Repository, accounts, domains, documentation. A client who cannot leave is not a happy client, just a trapped one.

06

Say no out loud

If a project is wrong for us, or we think you should spend the money elsewhere, you will hear it on the first call rather than in month three.

Handover

What you own the day we finish.

A client who cannot leave is not a happy client, just a trapped one. Everything below is handed over as a matter of course — you do not have to ask, and you do not have to pay extra for it.

Or keep us on monthly care

Included in every project

  • The source code in a repository you own, with its history intact
  • Every account — hosting, domain, analytics, payments — registered to you
  • Written documentation: how it is deployed, where things live, what to do when X happens
  • A recorded walkthrough of the admin area for whoever will run it
  • A list of what we would do next, whether or not we are the ones doing it
  • Our contact details, which keep working after the invoice is paid

Questions

About working with a studio of two.

Because the overhead of being bigger is paid for by clients, and in our experience it buys them very little. A second layer of project managers makes an agency easier to run, not the software better.

The trade-off is honest: we take on fewer projects at once, and occasionally we will tell you we cannot start for a few weeks.

Both of us have context on every project, and everything lives in your accounts rather than on someone's laptop, so work can continue and nothing is ever locked away.

For longer absences we tell you the dates up front and plan the schedule around them, rather than quietly going quiet.

Yes, as a rolling monthly care plan that you can cancel any month. It covers updates, monitoring, backups and a set amount of change work.

It is genuinely optional. A good number of clients take the handover pack and run things themselves, which is a perfectly good outcome.

We work remotely and arrange calls around whatever hours suit you. Updates are written down rather than delivered in meetings, so progress never depends on you being awake at the same time as us.
